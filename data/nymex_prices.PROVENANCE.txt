nymex_prices.csv and price_assumptions.csv  -  PROVENANCE
=========================================================

What it is
  Benchmark prices for the 12 months after the production data ends
  (May 2026 to April 2027), used only to turn forecast volumes into an
  indicative gross revenue. Prices are treated as given, not forecast.

Columns (nymex_prices.csv)
  date                  first day of the delivery month (YYYY-MM-DD)
  henry_hub_usd_mmbtu   NYMEX Henry Hub natural gas, USD per MMBtu
  wti_usd_bbl           WTI crude oil, USD per barrel
  price_type            "actual" = month already priced; "strip" = futures
                        price for a month not yet delivered

Sources
  Henry Hub, May to Nov 2026 (actual): NYMEX final contract settlement prices,
    NRG Energy, NYMEX Natural Gas Historical Prices,
    https://www.nrg.com/resources/energy-tools/nymex-settlement-history.html
    (accessed 6 November 2026).
  Henry Hub, Dec 2026 to Apr 2027 (strip): NYMEX settle prices on
    4 November 2026, NRG Energy daily market update,
    https://www.nrg.com/content/dam/nrg/web/en/multimedia/documents/pdfs/daily-market-update/11-05-26/nymex.pdf
  WTI, May to Sep 2026 (actual): monthly average Cushing, OK WTI spot price,
    U.S. Energy Information Administration, series RWTC,
    https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?n=PET&s=RWTC&f=M
  WTI, Oct 2026 (actual, partial): average of the three EIA weekly spot prices
    published for October (weeks ending 2, 9 and 16 October: 98.07, 96.88,
    99.93), EIA series RWTC weekly.
  WTI, Nov 2026 to Apr 2027 (strip): NYMEX crude settle on 4 November 2026
    from the same NRG update, which gives only three values: prompt 88.12
    (used for Nov and Dec 2026), Q1 2027 average 85.71 (Jan to Mar 2027) and
    12-month average 81.32 (used for Apr 2027 as an approximation).

Assumptions (price_assumptions.csv)
  btu_factor            1.05 MMBtu per MCF. Henry Hub is priced per MMBtu,
                        production is measured in MCF.
  gas_differential      -0.30 USD/MMBtu. Gas sold in South and East Texas
                        trades close to Henry Hub; for example the Houston
                        Ship Channel averaged 0.27 below Henry Hub in the
                        first half of 2023 (EIA, Today in Energy, 21 Aug 2023,
                        https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=60180).
  oil_differential      -5.00 USD/bbl below WTI for oil_bbl (oil and condensate),
                        a round assumption for field condensate and light
                        crude sold at the lease.
  scenarios             strip x 0.80 (low) and strip x 1.20 (high).
  These are assumptions, not the operator's realised prices or contract terms.

Revenue formula (gross, 8/8ths, before royalties, taxes and fees)
  gas revenue     = gas_mcf * btu_factor * (henry_hub_usd_mmbtu + gas_differential)
  oil revenue     = oil_bbl * (wti_usd_bbl + oil_differential)

Limitations
  - A single snapshot of the strip; futures prices move every day.
  - Revenue ranges built from volume prediction intervals reflect production
    uncertainty only; the low and high scenarios give a rough view of price risk.
